Tiny Post

Game support

For help with Tiny Post, contact burakkurt72@gmail.com. Parents and guardians can contact us on a child’s behalf.

Tell us your game version, device model and a short description of the problem. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, payment card details or full purchase receipts.

How do I play?

Open a level, arrange the delivery cards, select a route when offered, and start the van. A delivery can open a bridge, power a gate or change another part of the map. The delivery order affects which paths are available. Replay the tutorial from Settings.

Progress and offline play

The campaign and progress work offline. Progress and settings are saved on your device; there is no account or cloud save. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data may remove progress. Ads, purchases and purchase restoration need an internet connection.

Purchases and restoration

Remove Ads, Premium Pack and Explorer Pack are optional one-time purchases. Remove Ads stops automatic interstitial ads; player-initiated rewarded videos remain optional. The cosmetic packs do not change puzzle rules or unlock campaign levels. Use Restore Purchases in Settings or the shop with the same store account to restore eligible purchases. Verification must finish before content unlocks.

If an item is missing, reconnect, reopen the game and restore purchases. Contact support with the product name and approximate purchase date if it is still missing. Store refund requests go through Apple or Google Play.

Advertising

Interstitial ads appear only between rounds, subject to gameplay and time limits. Rewarded videos start only when you choose to watch. No ad availability is guaranteed; unavailable ads never prevent campaign play.

Language and accessibility

Change language, music, sound, vibration, reduced motion and lighter effects from Settings. Tiny Post supports English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese.
